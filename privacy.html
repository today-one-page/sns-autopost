1. 처리하는 정보

모든 정보는 운영자 PC에만 저장되며 별도 서버로 보내지 않습니다. 토큰은 연결을 해제하면 즉시 삭제하고, 업로드 기록은 운영자가 삭제할 때까지 보관합니다.

틱톡에 콘텐츠를 업로드하는 것 외에 어떤 제3자에게도 정보를 제공하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

  1. Information processed. When the operator connects their own TikTok account, the Tool processes only the following data provided by TikTok: the account identifier (open_id) and basic profile such as nickname; access and refresh tokens; and upload results (publish_id and processing status). No data about other people is collected.
  2. Purpose. The data is used only to identify the operator's account, upload drafts to the inbox, and check upload results.
  3. Storage and retention. All data is stored only on the operator's own computer and is not sent to any other server. Tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected; upload logs are kept until the operator deletes them.
  4. Sharing. No data is shared with any third party other than uploading content to TikTok.
  5. Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app's privacy and security settings under app permissions.
